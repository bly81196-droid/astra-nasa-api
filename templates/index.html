About ASTRA

ASTRA brings space weather information and astronaut health research data into one monitoring workspace.

Solar events can help researchers understand changing space weather conditions. They are not direct measurements of an astronaut's radiation dose.

Data Sources & Scientific Notes

Space weather events are retrieved through NASA DONKI. Biological research datasets from NASA OSDR are a separate data category and do not represent direct radiation dosimetry.
